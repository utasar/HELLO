Introduction
"Hi, my name is Utsav. When I hear the word diversity, I think about people being different from each other in culture, language, background, and experiences. I am originally from Nepal, and I have personally seen diversity through different ethnic groups, languages, and traditions in my home country. To me, diversity means understanding and respecting these differences. It is important because it helps people learn from each other, think more openly, and work better together as a team."

Diversity Landscape
"If I had to describe today's diversity landscape in one phrase, I would say in progress. This means diversity is improving, but it is not perfect yet. According to Northouse, perspectives on diversity have changed a lot over the years, and organizations today are making more effort to include different people. However, challenges like unconscious bias, cultural misunderstanding, and unequal opportunities still exist. After completing the Cultural Diversity Awareness Questionnaire, I reflected on how my own awareness can still grow. Leaders today need to be open-minded, culturally aware, and willing to keep learning. Diversity is moving forward, but leaders still have important work to do."

Inclusion Component
"The inclusion component that is most important to me personally is belongingness. According to Ferdman and his colleagues, belongingness means feeling genuinely accepted and part of a group — not just present, but truly valued. This relates to me deeply because when I first came to the United States from Nepal, I experienced what it feels like to be in a new environment where you are not sure if you belong. That feeling was difficult. But over time, through supportive leaders and colleagues, I began to feel more included and accepted. That sense of belonging gave me the confidence to speak up, share my ideas, and contribute more fully. As a future leader, I want to create environments where every person — no matter their background — feels like they truly belong."

Conclusion
"In conclusion, this reflection helped me understand that diversity is about our differences, but inclusion is about making sure every person feels accepted and valued. As a leader, I want to respect different backgrounds and create a positive environment for everyone around me. Thank you so much for watching — I really appreciate it."
